MEET YOUR DENTIST

Meet Dr. Daniel Johnson

Family & Cosmetic Dentist · Founder of Bright Smiles

Dr. Johnson began practicing dentistry years before opening Bright Smiles. Over time, many of his patients continued coming back, not just for the dental care, but because they felt listened to, understood, and genuinely looked after.

Today, Bright Smiles is built around that same approach. Dr. Johnson cares for patients of all ages, from children coming in for their first visits to parents and grandparents who want a dentist they can trust with the whole family.

He believes good dental care should feel calm, honest, and personal. That means taking time to explain what’s going on, answering questions without rushing, and helping every patient feel comfortable before treatment begins.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your questions, answered

We’ve answered common questions to help you feel confident before booking your visit.

Will I know the cost before treatment starts?

Yes. We’ll explain your treatment options, estimated costs, and payment details clearly before moving forward with care. We also accept most major dental insurance plans and can help verify your benefits before your appointment.

What if I’m not sure what treatment I need?

No problem. Book a general appointment and our team will help guide you to the right care based on your needs.

Do you offer same-week appointments?

We do our best to accommodate same-week visits whenever availability allows, especially for urgent concerns.

Can I book for my child or family member?

Yes. You can book an appointment for your child or another family member. Just choose the visit type and include their details when booking.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, your dental insurance information if applicable, and any recent dental records or X-rays if you have them.
